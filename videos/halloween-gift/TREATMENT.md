# Treatment: A Halloween Gift

Three structurally different candidates, written independently before any earlier film or
template was studied for this video, then scored by two judges (audience and craft;
feasibility and rules). Each has the arc in beats (40-ish bars of 3/4 at 120 bpm, 24 fps),
its image system, its sound idea and what could go wrong.

## Candidate A: the storybook
*A moonlit pop-up storybook knocks from the inside; the gift is its last page.*

1. **Hook (0:00–0:04.5).** A cloth storybook on a dark desk beside a small jack-o'-lantern;
   lightning lights the title *A Halloween Gift / 万圣节礼物*. The closed book knocks from
   inside; cyan light leaks from the pages.
2. **The valley (to 0:12).** The cover bursts open; a pop-up valley unfolds: castle on a
   floating rock, moon, a witch on a wire, the giant jack-o'-lantern. Paper Clawd springs
   up in a crooked witch hat with an ember wand and a pumpkin pail full of envelopes.
3. **The graveyard (to 0:18).** A page turn: tombstones, Rackham trees with knot-eyes,
   hanging tissue ghosts that try to peek into the pail.
4. **The gates and the last house (to 0:24).** Paper flats slide away; the crooked gates
   creak open; the path leads to the last lit house.
5. **Knock, knock (to 0:30).** A knock in "shave-and-a-haircut"; silence where "two bits"
   should be. The door flap lifts: a neighbour in a bedsheet-ghost costume with candy.
6. **The gift (to 0:42).** A pop-up letter rises: *A GIFT FOR YOU*. Ding-ding (the missing
   "two bits"): its slats flip to *ACCOUNT SUSPENDED*, a red 封号 seal thunks down, and every
   pumpkin in the world goes out.
7. **Trick, then treat (to 0:49.5).** The sheet sags; the user still drops a candy into the
   pail, which relights. Clawd winks and trots on to the next window.
8. **The book closes (to 1:00).** Slam, back on the desk, a 完 seal and the parody notice;
   the closed book knocks again.

**Image system.** Everything is paper standing up in a book, lit from behind like a
paper-lantern lightbox; scene changes are page turns, pop-ups and sliding flats; every word
is printed paper. The reference painting's palette: a teal world where only pumpkins are
warm, maroon near and teal far. Clawd and the letter are the only true rectangles: a warm
box and a cold box.
**Sound.** A D-minor waltz on pizzicato and tuba under a celesta and music box, a formant
ghost choir and a theremin; two dead silences; the notification ding completes the knock;
a Chopin funeral-march sting on the seal; a music box that winds down.
**What could go wrong.** The letter must read as an *email* inside a paper world; the page
turns can slow the gag; Chinese text and decor must be right; paper can look flat instead
of eerie.

## Candidate B: the long descent
*One unbroken moonlit take falls from the moon to the only warm doorstep; the film's first
cut is the email.*

1. **Hook (0:00–0:04.5).** The moon and the castle; bats pour at the lens; a witch crosses.
2. **The descent (to 0:16.5).** A crane through fog and past hanging ghost sheets whose
   eyes open and follow the camera.
3. **The graveyard walk (to 0:28.5).** Still in the same take: Clawd waddles past the RIP
   stone (and tips his hat to it), the cauldron and the creaking gates.
4. **The approach (to 0:36).** The house becomes a home: lanterns, couplets, 福. A knock;
   two bolts clack back: "two bits!"
5. **The gift (to 0:40.5).** The door opens on the sheet-ghost user; Clawd hands over a
   sealed envelope.
6. **The first cut (to 0:46.5).** An insert: the letter springs open, *ACCOUNT SUSPENDED*,
   the 信封 seal becomes the 封号 stamp.
7. **Locked off (to 0:54).** Back in the two-shot, the warm light gone; the sheet deflates;
   Clawd moves on with more envelopes.
8. **The ascent (to 1:00).** The camera whips back to the moon; a bat carries off the
   "Appeal" button.

**Image system.** The camera as a falling moonbeam; three sets joined by hidden wipes; the
first cut, the first silence and the first cold light land on the same frame.
**Sound.** The waltz never stops until the cut; its stopping is the suspension.
**What could go wrong.** Forty seconds before the gag; Clawd appears late; invisible seams,
a crane through cloth and thin bars on a long truck are the riskiest render of the three.

## Candidate C: behind the door
*Told from inside a warm house; the email drains the warmth out of the room.*

1. **Hook (0:00–0:06).** A fisheye peephole view of a foggy porch; a bat dives at the lens.
2. **Inside (to 0:13.5).** A coder in a bedsheet-ghost costume with a candy bowl and a
   laptop; the moonlit Halloween world seen through the window.
3. **The approach in fragments (to 0:21).** Small legs and a glowing pail in the fog; a
   huge hat shadow on the curtain.
4. **Knock (to 0:28.5).** Five knocks, silence, the peephole: tiny Clawd.
5. **The threshold (to 0:34.5).** The door opens; cold night pours across warm floorboards.
6. **The gift (to 0:43.5).** The envelope unfolds on the threshold: ding-ding, *ACCOUNT
   SUSPENDED*, 封号; the warm room drains cold and the user is "ghosted".
7. **Grace (to 0:49.5).** The user drops a candy into the pail anyway and closes the door.
8. **Kicker and end (to 1:01.5).** A far house's door flips cold with a faint ding-ding;
   the crane rises to the moon.

**Image system.** A frame inside every frame (peephole, window, curtain, doorway, screen);
warm inside, cold outside; the punchline erases the line.
**Sound.** Room tone against wind; real silences; the ding as the missing "two bits".
**What could go wrong.** Clawd's walk through the world is mostly seen through glass; two
full environments, fifteen shots and cloth acting under a sheet are expensive.

## Choice
**Candidate A**, chosen by the user on 2026-11-03 (both judges ranked it first, 54 points
each: the clearest gag that reads with no context, the only candidate that keeps the
reference's "only pumpkins are warm" rule, and the cheapest to make beautiful on the
reference laptop). The user also chose bilingual captions and the programmer couplet
代码千行一次过 / 账号百年永不封 · 横批 一路绿灯.

Ideas grafted from B and C, and fixes the judges required:
- The pumpkin wax seal stamped "@" cracks open, and its halves fly back together as the
  封号 seal (from B).
- A spring-wire bat snatches the "Appeal · 申诉" tab from the letter and carries it across
  the end card (B, C).
- The user keeps their dignity: after the sag they still drop a candy into Clawd's pail,
  which relights it; Clawd never picks anything up. The sheet goes "ghosted" (cyan
  eyeholes) and recovers when the door closes (C).
- The letter flips in 3D, then crossfades to a crisp DOM layer outside the post chain (C).
- The cold snap is made with lights and emissives, never a global white-balance shift, so
  Clawd stays on-model (C).
- A bundle of identical envelopes shows in the pail from the first spread, and a far window
  flips cold with its own ding-ding at the exit: an automated round, not a grudge (B, C).
- The letter rises from a pull-tab in the door spread, not a page turn over the puppets;
  scene changes are capped at two page turns, one slide and one close; the gift appears by
  about 0:32 and the ding lands at 0:36.
- Clawd glances at the lens once during the first silence (B).

## After the treatment
The film was made as Candidate A, but its picture changed in production. The first picture
(V1) was built as written above: paper sets on twos, page turns and a slide. On 2026-11-04 the
user chose one continuous 3D storybook world instead (V2: the world grows out of the page, one
camera journey with no page turn, sliding flat or cut, everything on ones). At the lookdev
checkpoint on 2026-11-06 the user kept the email's new wording ("Your Claude account has been
buried.", signed "The Graveyard Shift") in place of *ACCOUNT SUSPENDED*. Story, pacing, gag,
score and the Chinese strings stayed as approved. STORYBOARD.md describes the published film.
