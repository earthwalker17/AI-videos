# Storyboard: A Halloween Gift

40 bars of a 3/4 waltz at 120 bpm, 24 fps: a beat is 12 frames, a bar 36, the film 1440
frames (60.0 s). Times are film time; the final has no leader (the approved review cut carried a
12-frame test card before frame 0). The picture is one continuous world seen by one camera
journey with no cut, so each row is a beat of that journey, not a separate shot. The source of
truth for every frame is `src/cues.ts` (events), `src/film/beats.ts` (what the world does on each
cue), `src/film/journey.ts` (the camera) and `src/film/blocking.ts` (Clawd and the neighbour);
everything moves on ones.
