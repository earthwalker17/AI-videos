frames (60.0 s). Times are film time, after the review cut's 12-frame test card. The source
of truth for every frame is `src/cues.ts` (events) and `src/film/timeline.ts` (camera, book
mechanics); actors are blocked in `src/film/actors.ts`. Actors move on twos; the camera on ones.
