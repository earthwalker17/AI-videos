# AI use

- **What AI did:** the picture and the score are generated by code that an AI coding agent
  (Claude Code) wrote and revised under the creator's direction: the 3D world, the camera, the
  animation, the waltz and the sound effects. The creator chose the story and the visual
  reference, decides at every approval gate (treatment, lookdev, review cut, final) and listens
  to the score.
- **What no AI did:** no generative image, video, music or voice model was used. There is no
  photographic, recorded or sampled real person, voice or place; the instrument samples are
  VSCO 2 CE (CC0) recordings played by the film's own sampler.
- **YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" setting** (Studio, "AI use"): YouTube asks for it
  when realistic content is generated or meaningfully altered (real people, events or places,
  realistic scenes) and exempts clearly unrealistic, fully animated content. This film is a
  stylised animation of a cartoon character, so the setting is not required; the description
  says how the film was made. Whether YouTube's newer note on AI-generated music covers a
  background score written as code is unclear (references/youtube-publishing.md): the
  creator decides at upload.
