# A Halloween Gift

On Halloween night, Clawd set out with a gift for the last house still awake. A one-minute
storybook film: a book on a desk bursts open, a moonlit world of graveyards, crooked gates and
hanging ghosts grows out of its page, and the gift turns out to be... an email.

Made entirely in code, picture and score: a 3D world built procedurally with React Three Fiber
under Remotion, an original waltz and foley synthesised in TypeScript. Source, workflow and
every render step: https://github.com/earthwalker17/AI-videos/tree/main/videos/halloween-gift

Credits: VSCO 2 Community Edition samples by Versilian Studios (CC0); a quotation from Chopin's
Piano Sonata No. 2 (public domain); fonts IM Fell English, Noto Serif SC and Inter (OFL). Made
with Claude Code.
