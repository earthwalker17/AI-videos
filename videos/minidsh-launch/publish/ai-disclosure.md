# AI use

- **What AI did.** An AI coding agent (Claude Code, Claude Opus 5.5) did the following under the
  creator's direction:
  - wrote and revised the code that makes the picture and the score: the capture tools, the
    renderer, the staging of the captures, the type, the camera, every word on screen (the cue
    sheet) and the music;
  - drove the live product sessions (it typed the task and the follow-up with scripts) and chose
    the stills;
  - wrote three treatment candidates and recommended one.

  Agent reviewers (frame critics, first-time viewers with no context, a facts auditor) checked the
  cut; their findings were fixed or shown to the creator.
- **The product's footage.** The terminal, browser and desktop footage is real MiniDSH 2.0.0 output.
  The text the agent writes inside it (its replies, its reasons) comes from the model MiniDSH
  called, DeepSeek (deepseek-flash), unedited. The film shortens waits and never changes a state.
- **What the creator did:**
  - made MiniDSH;
  - chose the subject, the brief, the treatment, the panes' theme and the text size at the look
    check;
  - decided at every approval gate (treatment, review cut, final).
- **What no AI did.** No generative image, video, music or voice model made any of the film. Every
  sound is synthesised by code.
- **The thumbnail:** recorded here when it is added (see the release routine).
- **YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" setting:** the film shows real software and abstract
  animation, with no realistic person, place or event, so the setting is not required for the
  picture. The creator makes the call at upload.
