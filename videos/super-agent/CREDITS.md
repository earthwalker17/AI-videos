# Credits: Who Routes the Agents?

Everything in the picture is drawn by code in `src/`: a lit WebGL world (three.js geometry, shaders
and canvas-text textures: the Register Desk) under a DOM text layer, laid out and moved frame by
frame. Round 1's lookdev stills (DOM text and inline SVG) are kept in `src/lookdev/` for the record. The score
and its sound effects are composed and synthesised by code in `audio/`. The film uses no
third-party media: no images, footage, samples, models or textures. Its one committed image,
`assets/dither-256.png`, is a seeded noise tile generated by `src/lookdev/ditherTile.ts`.
