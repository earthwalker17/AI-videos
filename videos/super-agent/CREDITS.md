# Credits: Who Routes the Agents?

Everything in the picture is drawn by code in `src/`: DOM text and inline SVG (the route map, its
lines, rings, boxes and tokens, the sketch strokes), laid out and moved frame by frame. The score
and its sound effects are composed and synthesised by code in `audio/`. The film uses no
third-party media: no images, footage, samples, models or textures. Its one committed image,
`assets/dither-256.png`, is a seeded noise tile generated by `src/lookdev/ditherTile.ts`.

renders, which the final does not have, is set in Inter (`@fontsource-variable/inter` 5.3.0,
OFL-1.1) by the shared kit.
