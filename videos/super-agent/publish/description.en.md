**Title:** Who Routes the Agents? | DSH Super-agent Architecture Explanation

What happens when you run multiple AI agents in parallel? Someone has to manage their tasks, context and progress, who is usually you.

This 2min48s visual explainer breaks down the Super-agent architecture proposed by the DeepSeek Harness team earlier this month: a long-lived Router coordinating independent Workers, supported by event filtering, task subscriptions, batching, and context management.

It also covers the authors' experimental results, the trade-offs, and what's still proposed or unresolved. This is an architectural exploration, not a finished product.

This video itself took three iterations to get right.

The first version was too simplistic: mostly flat 2D diagrams. When I asked Opus 5.5 to improve visual quality, the second went too far in the opposite direction: elaborate 3D objects, excessive details, and a cluttered composition.

For this final version, I switched to Fable 5.2, provided clearer visual references, and focused on hierarchy, continuity, and storytelling rather than complexity.

The result is a cleaner, more coherent visualization built entirely with code: WebGL-rendered frames, programmatic music, and no voiceover or generative video models.
