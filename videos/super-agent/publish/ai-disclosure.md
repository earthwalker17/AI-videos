# AI use

- **What AI did:** the picture and the score are generated by code that an AI coding agent
  (Claude Code, Claude Fable 5.2) wrote and revised under the creator's direction: the renderer,
  the scenes, the type, the camera, the script (every word on screen, in the cue sheet) and the
  music. AI agents also wrote the three treatment candidates and recommended the hybrid of them,
  and reviewed the cut (frame critics, first-time viewers with no context, a facts auditor, a
  motion reviewer): their findings were fixed before the creator's review, and three known weak
  spots remained and were shown to the creator. The research behind the film
  ([FACTS.md](../FACTS.md)) was gathered and cross-checked by the agent from the English article
  (a machine translation, read through a third-party mirror; the Chinese original was not read)
  and from the authors' open-source prototype.
- **What the creator did:** chose the subject and the brief, chose the hybrid treatment
  recommended from three candidates and the palette at a look check, and decides at every
  approval gate (treatment, review cut, final).
- **What no AI did:** no generative image, video, music or voice model was used in the film.
  There is no photographic, recorded or sampled person, voice or place in it: shapes, lines and
  type are drawn by code, and every sound is synthesised by code.
- **The thumbnail** (`thumbnail.jpg`) is a frame of the film itself, drawn by the same code; its
  provenance is in `thumbnail.provenance.json`. If the creator replaces it at upload, the record
  is updated with what was used.
- **YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" setting** (Studio, "AI use"): YouTube asks for it
  when realistic content is generated or meaningfully altered (real people, events or places,
  realistic scenes) and exempts clearly unrealistic, fully animated content. This film is an
  abstract animation of shapes and type, so the setting is not required for the picture; the
  description says how the film was made. Whether YouTube's note on AI-generated music covers a
  score written as code is unclear (references/youtube-publishing.md); the creator makes that
  call at upload.
