# AI use

- **What AI did:** the picture and the score are generated by code that an AI coding agent
  (Claude Code, with Opus 5.5) wrote and revised under the creator's direction: the Register
  Desk world, its camera and animation, the on-screen text and its layout, the score and its
  sound effects. AI agents also researched the article and the prototype, drafted the copy and
  reviewed the rendered frames as critics; every claim on screen is checked against its source
  in `FACTS.md`. The creator decided at every gate (the treatment and the production plan, the
  lookdev, the review cut) and on the choices between them (no voice, the look, the length, the
  wording of a line); the creator judged the score's sketches "right direction" and approved
  the review cut with its full score.
- **What no AI did:** no generative image, video, music or voice model is used in the film, and
  there is no voice. The film uses no third-party media: no photographs, footage, recordings,
  samples, 3D models or textures (`CREDITS.md`); its type is set in OFL fonts (IBM Plex Sans,
  Noto Sans SC, IBM Plex Mono). Every instrument and sound effect of the score is synthesised
  by code, not played from samples.
- **The thumbnail** (`thumbnail.jpg`) is rendered by the film's own code from its world
  (`src/thumbnail/`), with no generative image model.
- **YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" setting** (Studio, "AI use"): YouTube asks for it
  when realistic content is generated or meaningfully altered (real people, events or places,
  realistic scenes) and exempts clearly unrealistic content, animation in fully animated videos
  and production assistance such as scripts and thumbnails. This film is a stylised animated
  diagram, a desk of paper and machines, and shows no real person or place, so the setting is
  not required; the description says how the film was made. Whether YouTube's newer note on
  AI-generated music covers a background score synthesised by code that an AI agent wrote is
  unclear (references/youtube-publishing.md, read 2026-11-01, to be re-checked after
  2026-12-01); the creator makes that call at upload.
