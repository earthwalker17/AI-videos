# Storyboard: Who Routes the Agents?

One row per shot. The film is 84 bars at 120 bpm (2:48 at 60 fps); times are from its first frame
and come from the cue sheet ([`src/cues.ts`](src/cues.ts)), which also holds every word
([SCRIPT.md](SCRIPT.md)). One camera rides one stack: a steep look-down explains one mechanism on
one level, the edge-on view shows the whole stack between mechanisms. The marks keep one meaning
each ([TREATMENT.md](TREATMENT.md)): a dot is an event (a coral ring: a person sent it), a capsule
a whole task, a cell one session, a line history or a subscription; green light is a model running,
coral is you; solid is in the prototype, dashed is proposed, a gap is open.
