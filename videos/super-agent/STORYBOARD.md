**The look in one paragraph.** One dark slate desk at night (`#212322`, a near-neutral
green-graphite with static seeded tooth; corners never darken) under a fixed key light, where
every object casts an analytic footprint shadow unless it is vellum, pencil or in flight. Context
is paper: how much paper a part holds is the story. **Material is status**: enamel `#D9D5CA` on
graphite is built, vellum (a 14% fill with a dashed edge) is proposed, pencil `#8C8A85` is open,
and grey stock `#77756F` is the road not taken (counterfactual cards that rise, play and are
struck through). Vermilion `#E8603F` is only the request, its Thread cord and its route; sign-off
green `#8FD3A8` only your stamp and the return route; no navy, amber, blue or third hue. **The
emission rule** ("Light where it means (Recommended)", 2026-11-08/09): emission marks a live process,
never a thing. Only your lamp's bulb while it attends, a Worker's work slot while it computes and
one transit light on the single carrier in flight may emit; at most 3 blooming sources and 0.8% of
the frame; lines, cords, text and anything idle never do. Relationships take five forms and never
a bare connector: grooves (whole hand-overs), overhead cords (Thread subscriptions), flights
(one-off events), tape (memory) and the reply chute (the only path to you). Notes sit lower-left
(EN Plex Sans 48, zh Noto Sans SC 40), labels are 40 px on hairline leaders, and while a note is
up the diagram action stays within y 72–868. Micro-print is texture only ("Micro-print as texture
(Recommended)", 2026-11-08/09: ≥ 18 px, never meant to be read); text meant to be read is ≥ 36 px.
The angle is the user's pick from the style frames ("Desk, render both angles (Recommended)",
2026-11-08/09): 15° long lens or straight down, bloom on or off.
