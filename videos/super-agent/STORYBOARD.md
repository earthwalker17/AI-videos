# Storyboard: Who Routes the Agents?

66 bars of 4/4 at 90 bpm, 30 fps: a beat is 20 frames, a bar 80, the film 5280 frames
(2:56.0). Frames are film frames, after the review cut's 15-frame test card. The picture is
one dark ink map (3840×2160 world units) under one camera that only translates and scales,
with no cut and one dissolve to the ground before the end card. Each row is therefore a shot
of that one journey: the stretch between two camera moves or structural changes. The
source of truth for timing and words is [src/cues.ts](src/cues.ts); the look (palette, type,
line grammar, world layout, motion grammar) is TREATMENT.md's
[Art direction](TREATMENT.md#art-direction-dark-ink-map), and the copy is in
[SCRIPT.md](SCRIPT.md).

**The look in one paragraph.** A flat ground `#202226` with a faint dot grid and a baked,
seeded dither tile; no glow, bloom, blur, particles, vignette or serif. Ink `#E8E6E0` is built
structure and EN text, `#B9B7B1` the zh line, sketch `#75736D` act one, the chart rails and
what is open; dashed ink is proposed. Vermilion `#E8603F` is only the request, its route, its
thread and its follow-up; sign-off green `#8FD3A8` only your sign-off and the cased return
route. Risk is 45° hatching, never a new hue. Notes sit lower-left (EN Plex Sans Medium 48,
zh Noto Sans SC 40), and while a note is up the diagram action stays within y 72–868.

sign-off and the summary map, 5.3 s with no text; the sound rest from bar 51 beat 3, 4.0 s)
and the decay after the end card. One silence: bar 48 beats 1–2, before the intrusion. Camera moves: two eases out (bar 3, bar 51), trucks and eases (bars 11, 13, 15),
the ride along the trunk (bar 19), pans and eases (bars 28, 30), the glide south (bar 37),
the rise back to the map (bar 46), the push in (bar 27) and the bookend return (bar 57); each
lasts 1 or 2 bars and starts on a downbeat. Every line clears its reading hold in both
languages (SCRIPT.md). The only still stretch of 3 s or more is the end card, declared as a
hold.
