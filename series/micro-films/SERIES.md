# Micro Films

> Short conceptual and philosophical films: consciousness, humanity, the evolution
> of AI, the future, civilization, meaning.

Status: active · First video: none yet

## Promise
A few minutes that leave the viewer with an image and a question they keep
thinking about. A micro film argues through images, sound and time, not through a
lecture.

## Editorial principles
- **Image first.** If a line of narration only describes what is on screen, cut it.
  Words, when present, add what images cannot.
- **One governing metaphor.** Each film commits to one central image system and
  develops it; no montage of unrelated beautiful shots.
- **Restraint.** Silence, stillness and negative space are tools. Every film earns at
  least one moment of quiet.
- **Avoid the clichés of the genre:** glowing brains, robots shaking hands, binary
  rain, swirling galaxies as a stand-in for "big ideas".
- **Every film is new.** The treatment explores three structurally different
  candidates before choosing one (`TREATMENT.md`), so films do not converge on a
  template.
- **Ambiguity is allowed; vagueness is not.** The film may leave the question open,
  but the maker knows what is being asked.

## Craft hypotheses (to confirm with the first film)
- Engines: the web engine for shader-driven and generative imagery (WebGL/WebGPU),
  Remotion for typography and compositing; Blender for hero assets when needed.
- Generative AI plates and voices are likely in some films (hybrid), handled as
  immutable sourced inputs with provenance sidecars and a rights class.
- Sound carries most of the emotion: a designed soundscape and score; narration
  optional and sparse.
- Length: 2–6 minutes, 16:9 (a 9:16 cut when the composition allows it).

## Policies
- Generative AI: allowed for plates, textures and voices, recorded per asset
  (model, prompt, terms, rights class) and disclosed on YouTube when the policy
  requires it. Never used to fake a real person or a real event.
- Quotations and references to thinkers are attributed and checked.
